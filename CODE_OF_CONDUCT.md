# Code of Conduct

Kea welcomes contributors of every background and experience level. Be respectful,
patient and constructive in issues, pull requests and other project spaces.

- Critique ideas and code, not people. Assume good intent and accept corrections.
- Help newcomers participate. Respect differing perspectives and accessibility needs.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, make sexual advances, or publish another
  person's private information. Trolling and personal attacks are not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful contributions, close or lock discussions,
and restrict participation when needed. Responses should be proportionate to the
behavior, its impact and any repeated violations.

To report a concern, ask [@ManuelZierl](https://github.com/ManuelZierl) for a private
contact channel in a project issue, without including personal or incident details.
Share relevant context privately once a channel is agreed. Reports will be handled
as confidentially as practical.
